# Mother's Day Yearbook 🌸

Welcome to the Mother's Day Yearbook project!

## 💝 Why I Made This
I created this website to showcase my love and appreciation for my mom. It's a special, interactive digital yearbook designed to hold cherished memories, photos, and messages. I wanted to build something unique and personal to celebrate her.

## 🌟 Feel Free to Use It!
If you're looking for a heartfelt way to surprise your own mom, you are more than welcome to use this website! 
You can customize it, add your own photos, write your own personal letters, and make it a beautiful gift for her.

## 🚀 How to Use
1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open `mothers-day.html` in your web browser.
3. Customize the images and text to personalize it for your mom.
4. Share it with her and make her day!
